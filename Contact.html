Common Inquiries

How early should we reserve our wedding date?

Due to our policy of accepting a limited number of weddings per season to maintain bespoke quality, peak season dates (October through March) typically book 6 to 12 months in advance.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes, nearly 60% of our commissions are destination celebrations. We travel across Rajasthan, Goa, Himachal, Kerala, as well as international destinations in Europe and Southeast Asia.

When will our finished photographs be delivered?

You will receive an editorial sneak peek of 50 to 80 photographs within 7 days following the wedding. The complete master gallery of high-resolution images is delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.
