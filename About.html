The Story Behind The Lens

About Us

We are a story-driven wedding photography & films studio rooted in the mountains — capturing weddings with heart, soul, and an uncompromising eye for genuine emotion.

Shailesh Hendre, a passionate visual storyteller, started following his love for capturing people's stories more than 8 years ago. Growing up witnessing the vibrant colours and rituals of Indian weddings, photography became more than a profession — it became a calling.

MountainClicks was born from that deep-rooted passion, and over the years a team of creative minds joined who share the same love for authentic, unscripted moments.

Backed by a passionate team of creative professionals, we specialize in documenting wedding stories in their purest, most authentic form — blending rich traditions with a modern, candid touch.

We believe that being friends with our clients and making them feel comfortable gives us an upper hand. It helps us bring the best in you, which results in a magical output.

Our Approach

“Frames of Nostalgia, Stories of Love”

We love to be inspired by your personalities and your wedding to tell your individual story — because every story, every couple, and every wedding is unique and different.

With us you will get a wedding reportage with authentic pictures, heartfelt films, real feelings and memories.

We love to travel, and we are ready to go anywhere to capture special stories and meet great people.

We're in this to give you something no one else can —
a memory that's all heart, no templates.

What Guides Our Work

Pure Documentary Style

We blend rich traditions with a modern, candid touch. Instead of forced poses, we capture unscripted smiles, tender tears, and raw emotions as they naturally unfold.

Like Family, Not Vendors

Driven by Relationships

For us, success isn't measured by accolades — it's measured by the trust we build, the comfort we provide, and the lasting relationships we form with our clients.

Let's Tell Your Story Together

Every celebration is unique. We would love to learn about your plans and help preserve the emotions, traditions, and moments that matter most.
